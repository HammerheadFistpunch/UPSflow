Here's every telemetry attribute available on your `UPSFlowRiver2` device objects (via `getattr(device, name)`), pulled from the pinned eflib source plus your own overrides:

**Battery / power flow**
- `battery_level` — % state of charge
- `input_power` — total input watts (all sources combined)
- `output_power` — total output watts
- `cell_temperature`

**AC**
- `ac_input_power` — AC watts in
- `ac_output_power` — AC watts out
- `ac_input_voltage` — your override, scaled voltage
- `ac_input_current` — your override, scaled current
- `ac_ports` — bool, AC outlets enabled
- `ac_xboost` — bool, X-Boost enabled
- `ac_charging_speed` / `ac_charging_power_min` / `ac_charging_power_max`

**DC / Solar**
- `dc_port_input_power` — raw XT60 port watts (not mode-gated)
- `solar_input_power` — derived, only nonzero when `dc_mode == SOLAR`
- `car_input_power` — derived, only nonzero when `dc_mode == CAR`
- `dc_mode` — current `DCMode` (AUTO/SOLAR/CAR/UNKNOWN)
- `dc_12v_port` — bool, 12V port enabled
- `dc12v_output_power`
- `dc_charging_max_amps` / `dc_charging_current_max`

**USB outputs**
- `usbc_output_power`
- `usba_output_power`

**Energy backup / limits**
- `energy_backup` — bool
- `energy_backup_battery_level`
- `battery_charge_limit_min` / `battery_charge_limit_max`
- `remaining_time_charging` / `remaining_time_discharging`

**Connection/identity (from `DeviceBase`, used elsewhere in your script)**
- `is_connected`
- `name`, `serial_number`, `address`

Your `telemetry_device()`/`format_device()` functions in `upsflow.py` currently only surface a handful of these (battery, AC, solar, totals, output) — anything else here you can pull the same way with `value(d, "field_name")`.
